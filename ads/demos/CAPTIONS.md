## 2. ServeFlow demo

---
☕ How ServeFlow works, from the table to the kitchen.

Online ordering for cafés and restaurants, with table QR codes and 0% commission.

1️⃣ Your customer scans the QR code on their table. The table number is filled in for them
2️⃣ Not sure what to order? They ask the AI menu helper
3️⃣ They pick the size, milk and extras. The price updates as they go
4️⃣ They pay by GCash, bank transfer or at the counter
5️⃣ The order lands on your kitchen board: verify the payment, then Preparing → Ready
6️⃣ Your customer sees "Your order is ready!" on their phone
7️⃣ Running low? Your AI kitchen co-pilot prepares changes like "set ensaymada to 10 left", and waits for you to tap Confirm

## 3. StayFlow demo

---
🛏️ How StayFlow works, from your guest's phone to your dashboard.

Direct bookings for hotels, resorts and rentals, with an AI booking agent.

1️⃣ Guests book on your own website, with no booking fees
2️⃣ They add their dates and see which rooms are free
3️⃣ They send a stay request. Nothing is charged, and nothing is booked yet

6️⃣ Your guest's booking page shows the confirmed stay and how to pay the downpayment by GCash or bank
7️⃣ Same room, same dates? StayFlow stops it. No more double bookings
