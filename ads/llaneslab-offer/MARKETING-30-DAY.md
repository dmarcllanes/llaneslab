**Goal:** Book free consultations with shops and small businesses, hotels and rentals, and clinics.
**Dates:** Mon 30 Nov 2026 to Tue 29 Dec 2026 (prep in Week 0, Fri 27 – Sun 29 Nov)
**Time needed:** About 1 hour a day (30 min content + 30 min outreach)

Rules for every post and message:

## 7. Outreach scripts

**Focus your DMs this month: 7 of every 10 go to hotel and rental hosts.** Ten DMs a day split three ways is too thin to learn from. Hosts with 2+ listings are easy to find, have the same repeat admin (guest questions, dates, deposits), and already pay for tools. The other 3 a day go to shops, then clinics. Posts still cover all three audiences in the order of the weekly themes. At the Friday review, if another group is replying more, move the 7 to them for January.

**Who to message (10 a day):**
- **Shops:** local shops with an active Facebook page, online sellers, small distributors
- **Hotels & rentals:** Airbnb / Booking.com hosts with 2+ listings, small resorts, guesthouses
- **Clinics:** independent clinics, dental and physical therapy practices, solo practitioners

Find them through Google Maps, Facebook pages, LinkedIn search ("owner" + industry + city), and Airbnb host profiles. Track each one in a simple sheet: Name | Business | Channel | Date sent | Follow-up 1 | Follow-up 2 | Reply | Booked?

**Always personalise the first line.** Mention one real thing about their business. Without that, it reads as spam.
